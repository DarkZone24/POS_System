## 💡 Quick Reference Guides

### Password Strength
**Read**: [`USER_SECURITY_GUIDE.md`](./USER_SECURITY_GUIDE.md) - "Create Strong Password"

**Quick Rule**:
- 8+ characters
- Uppercase (A-Z)
- Lowercase (a-z)
- Number (0-9)
- Special (!@#$%^&*)

---

### Payment Methods
**Read**: [`README.md`](./README.md) - "Payment Methods"

**6 Options**:
1. 💵 Cash
2. 💳 Credit Card
3. 💳 Debit Card
4. 📱 GCash
5. 📱 Maya
6. 📲 QR PH

---

### Validation Rules
**Read**: [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) - "Input Validation"

**Key Points**:
- Barcode: Alphanumeric, max 100 chars
- Price: 0.00 - 999,999.99
- Quantity: 1-9999
- Username: 3-20 alphanumeric
